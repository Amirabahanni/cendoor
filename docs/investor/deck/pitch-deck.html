The solution

Orders confirmed before you ship, in your customer’s language

1. Build

Describe the shop in a chat. The AI designs a site made for you, with your photos, products, delivery and stock. Change anything by talking.

2. Confirm

The AI takes the order in Darija, French, Arabic or English, then asks the customer to confirm. The seller approves with one tap. Risky orders are flagged.

3. Deliver and grow

Labels, tracking updates, cash on delivery tracked, failed deliveries chased. Offers to past customers and ads from one button.

Honest status  Built and tested in the demo. Sending through WhatsApp, Instagram and ads needs approval from Meta; carrier tracking is simulated.

Why now

The channels are there. The tools for small sellers are not.

Social commerce is the storefront

35.5M internet users (92%) and 22.8M social identities in Morocco. Instagram and TikTok audiences grew about 20% in a year.

DataReportal Digital 2026; Morocco World News summary (growth figures not verified at source).

WhatsApp is the order desk

Orders start in a DM and end on a doorstep with cash. Confirmation is the step that decides whether the parcel is accepted.

Rule-based understanding today, language models for the hard cases. Competitors have started: Babliy launched in June 2026.

Go to market

Win trust one seller at a time, then let the link spread

1. Talk

Message 10 sellers a day by DM, one by one. Ask how they confirm orders today. No pitch.

2. Pilot

3 to 10 sellers, free for 30 days, with approve-before-send and the founder on their WhatsApp.

3. Founding 50

99 MAD locked for 12 months, refund if no order is confirmed in 30 days.

4. Spread

Every shop link says “powered by Cendoor”; sellers refer sellers; short Darija videos on Reels and TikTok.

Budget about 2,000 MAD a month. Most a seller is worth paying to win: about 228 MAD at 99 MAD (3-month payback).
