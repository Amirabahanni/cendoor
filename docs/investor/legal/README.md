# Legal drafts: read this first

These four documents are **drafts written to get a lawyer started**. They are not legal advice and they have not been reviewed by a Moroccan lawyer. **Do not publish them or ask real sellers or customers to accept them until a lawyer has reviewed them**, adjusted them to the final company form, and confirmed the points marked `[CHECK]`.

| File | For whom | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `terms-of-service.md` | sellers who sign up | the contract for using Cendoor |
| `privacy-policy.md` | customers of the sellers' shops, and visitors | what data is collected, why, and the person's rights |
| `data-processing-agreement.md` | each seller | splits responsibility for customer data (seller = controller, Cendoor = processor) |
| `pilot-agreement.md` | the first 3 to 10 sellers | a short, plain agreement for the free 30-day pilot |

## What a lawyer must settle before real use

1. **The company form and name** that signs these (the company is not registered yet).
2. **Moroccan data-protection law (Law 09-08, CNDP).** The CNDP's own page says commercial messages need the recipient's prior consent, and that the processing must be declared to the CNDP before it starts ([CNDP, "Prospection"](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/fiche_CNDP_Prospection_RT_fr.pdf)). Who files the declaration (Cendoor, each seller, or both) and the exact consent wording at checkout are open `[CHECK]`.
3. **Transfers abroad.** If the Claude AI features are used, customer messages are sent to an AI provider outside Morocco; hosting may also be abroad. Whether the CNDP must authorise transfers is a `[CHECK]`.
4. **Consumer-protection rules** for guarantees, cash on delivery, returns and advertising claims, and who is responsible for what the seller promises. `[CHECK]`
5. **WhatsApp and Meta rules.** Business-initiated messages need approved templates; ads are subject to Meta's advertising policies. Sellers need to be told they are responsible for what they send.
6. **Limits of liability** and the governing law and courts.
7. **Retention periods** (how long orders, chats and customer data are kept) and deletion on request.
