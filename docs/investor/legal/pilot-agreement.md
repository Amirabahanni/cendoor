# Pilot agreement (free 30 days) — DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer

A short, plain agreement for the first sellers who try Cendoor on real orders. A lawyer should review it, and it should be signed by the registered company once it exists. Fill the `[brackets]`.

**Between:** [Cendoor legal name or the founder as a sole trader, address] ("Cendoor") and [seller name, shop, phone, email] ("the seller").

## 1. What this is
For **30 days from [start date]** Cendoor lets the seller use the service **free of charge** on real orders, so both sides can learn whether it helps. It is a pilot: the product is still being built and may have mistakes.

## 2. What Cendoor does
- Sets up the shop and the order flow **with the seller** (about [2] hours of help).
- Keeps **approve-before-send on**: no message goes to a customer without the seller's tap, unless the seller chooses otherwise.
- Answers the seller's questions within [24] hours on [WhatsApp number].
- Fixes problems quickly and tells the seller what changed.

## 3. What the seller does
- Uses Cendoor for the shop's real orders for the 30 days, with real customers.
- Tells Cendoor **before the pilot** the seller's usual numbers (orders per week, how many are refused or never answered, hours spent confirming) and shares the same numbers at days 7, 14 and 30, so a fair before/after can be made.
- Tells Cendoor about any wrong confirmation or bad message **the same day**.
- Obtains their customers' **consent for marketing messages** and respects "STOP".

## 4. Messages and WhatsApp
Until WhatsApp access is approved, Cendoor prepares the messages and the seller sends them from their own phone with one tap. The seller stays responsible for what is sent in their name.

## 5. Data
The seller's customers' data belongs to the seller. Cendoor handles it only to run the pilot, under the terms of the Data Processing Agreement (attached or to follow), keeps it secure as described in its security overview, and **deletes or returns it on request and at the end if the seller does not continue** (within [30] days). The pilot is not a reason to share data with anyone else.

## 5b. Reading conversations
The seller agrees that Cendoor may read the shop's conversations and orders **to fix problems and measure results**. Cendoor will not share them outside the team.

## 6. Results and quoting
Cendoor may use **anonymous, combined numbers** from the pilot (for example "the average refused-order rate went from X% to Y% across N sellers"). Cendoor will **only name the seller or quote them with their written permission**, which the seller can give or refuse at the end (tick one): ☐ named ☐ anonymous only.

## 7. No promise of results
Cendoor does not promise more sales or fewer refused orders. The seller can stop at any time, for any reason, by telling Cendoor.

## 8. After the pilot
If the seller wants to continue, they can sign up at the standard price or as a **Founding seller**: [99] MAD per month locked for [12] months, if they confirm before [date]. If not, the account is closed and data returned or deleted as in section 5. The seller owes nothing for the pilot.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential. This agreement is not exclusive: the seller may use other tools.
