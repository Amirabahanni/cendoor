## 2. What data we handle
| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Order data | name, phone number, city, address, items, price, delivery status | to take, confirm, ship and deliver your order |
| Conversation data | your messages to the shop's chat or WhatsApp, and the replies | to answer you and take your order |
| Delivery and cash data | carrier, tracking number, whether delivered or refused | to deliver and settle cash on delivery |
| Optional marketing data | whether you accept offers, whether you asked to stop | to send offers only if you agreed |
| Risk signal | a **scrambled (hashed) version** of your phone number with counts of delivered and failed orders across shops; no name or address | to protect sellers from fake or abusive orders `[CHECK: lawfulness and whether it needs separate consent or a CNDP authorisation]` |
| Website visit data | basic technical data and which shop link brought you | to run and secure the site, and show a seller which post brought an order |
| Seller account data | email, name, role, shop details | to run the account |

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive data such as health, religion or political views. Please do not send it in the chat.
