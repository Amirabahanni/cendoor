# Metrics: what to measure, how, and the pilot targets

Investors fund evidence, not plans. These are the numbers to collect from day one of the pilot, how each is defined, and the level that would count as a good early result. **Targets are proposals for the founder to adjust; none is a result.** There are no real results yet.

## The headline metrics (show these first)

| Metric | Definition | Where it comes from | Pilot target (30 days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed-order rate** | Orders confirmed by the customer ÷ orders placed | Orders table (status) | at least 15 points above the seller's own "before" |
| **Failed or refused delivery rate** | Failed deliveries ÷ shipped | Orders (`failed`) | at least 20% lower than the seller's "before" |
| **Hours saved per seller per week** | Seller's own estimate before vs after, checked by order counts | Interview + Home "time saved" | at least 3 hours |
| **Wrong-confirmation rate** | Confirmations the seller had to correct ÷ confirmations | Approve-mode edits, complaints | under 2% and no customer lost without a same-day fix |
| **Sellers who confirm ≥10 real orders** | Count | Orders by shop | at least 3 |
| **Written commitments to pay** | Signed notes at 99 MAD | founder log | at least 3 |

## Funnel (acquisition and activation)

| Metric | Definition | Level that means "change something" |
|---|---|---|
| Replies per 10 DMs | sellers who answer ÷ sellers contacted | under 2 of 10 |
| Trials per 100 link clicks | trial starts ÷ clicks on a post link | under 5 |
| Activation | trial sellers with a first real order in 7 days ÷ trial starts | under 50% |
| Trial to paid | paying after trial ÷ trial ends | under 25% (the panel's upper bound) |

## Business metrics (once billing exists)

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| MRR | paying sellers × plan price |
| ARPU | MRR ÷ paying sellers |
| Gross margin per seller | price minus messages, AI usage, payment fees (CFO estimate: about 126 MAD of 149) |
| Monthly churn | sellers lost ÷ sellers at start of month |
| CAC | marketing spend + founder selling time (priced) ÷ new paying sellers |
| Payback | CAC ÷ monthly contribution per seller (target under 3 months) |
| Break-even | fixed costs ÷ contribution per seller (CFO: 24 sellers at 149 MAD with no founder pay) |

## Product-health metrics

Messages answered without a human, share of conversations taken over by the seller, ad launches and delivered-order return on budget (Ads), campaign orders, STOP rate (opt-outs ÷ messages), support tickets per seller.

## How to collect them

1. A shared sheet per pilot seller: week-by-week orders, confirmed, refused, hours spent confirming, "before" values written in week 0.
2. Export from the dashboard (orders CSV) every Friday; paste the totals.
3. A 10-minute call at day 7, 14 and 30 using the questions in `research/launch.md`.
4. Keep the raw sheets; they are the evidence in the data room.

## Honest limits

With 3 to 10 sellers these are anecdotes, not statistics. Report the counts, name no one without permission, and show the "before" next to the "after" for each seller.
