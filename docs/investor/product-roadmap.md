# Product roadmap (12 months)

Principle from the board review: **one sharp thing first.** Cendoor wins by being the best at *getting cash-on-delivery orders confirmed and delivered* for small Moroccan sellers; the store builder, ads and campaigns support that. Dates are targets, not promises; each stage ends with a real-seller check, not a feature list.

## Where the product is today (built and tested, 114 automated checks)

AI shop builder (each shop designed from a description, edited by chat), customer chat that takes orders in Darija/French/Arabic/English, AI order confirmation, tracking page, cash-on-delivery tracking, failed-delivery recovery, post links with tracking, targeted campaigns, launching an ad from a button (dry run until connected), risk signals, copilot advice, roles, trial and plans (billing not connected).

**Not real yet:** WhatsApp sending through Meta, Instagram/TikTok inboxes, real carrier tracking, billing, the Claude-written pages mode (never run with a real key), ad sending to Meta's live servers.

## Stage 1: prove it with real sellers (weeks 0 to 6)

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Approve-before-send mode, with an always-approve option | Trust was the top reason simulated sellers said no |
| Customer export | Answers "who owns my data" |
| Per-shop kill switch | Stops a bad message fast |
| One real path: order, confirmation message, delivery status | The board's "one seam" |
| 3 to 10 pilot sellers on real orders; measure refused/failed orders before and after, and hours saved | The only evidence that matters |
| Test the Claude pages mode with a real API key and review its output | Credits can't be sold untested |
| Legal basics: business registration, lawyer review of terms and privacy, CNDP step | Needed before billing or messaging real customers |

**Exit check:** at least 3 pilot sellers confirm 10+ real orders each, at least 3 written commitments to pay, no serious incident.

## Stage 2: make it reliable and billable (months 2 to 4)

- WhatsApp Business verification and the first approved message templates (confirmation, delivery update, offer)
- Billing (card or local options) and the credits flow; the per-confirmed-order plan if pilots ask for it
- One or two carrier integrations for real tracking
- Daily backups, monitoring, and a managed database decision
- Founding 50 launch (99 MAD locked 12 months), pricing test 99 vs 149

## Stage 3: grow the base (months 4 to 8)

- Meta ads connection approved for other sellers; delivered-order reports per ad
- Referral loop (a pilot seller invites another, both get a month)
- Darija speech-to-text for voice notes (evaluate before building)
- Shared risk signal improving as sellers join (only valuable at scale)
- Team seats and the Growth plan features

## Stage 4: widen (months 8 to 12)

- Instagram and TikTok message integrations if Meta/TikTok approve
- Second city/region focus, support hire (Darija speaker)
- Evaluate a similar cash-on-delivery market for expansion, only with evidence from Morocco first
- Revenue beyond subscription: per-order fees, payment/COD float, partner commissions (each needs a partner or licence)

## Deliberately not now

- TikTok ads automation (separate approval, slower)
- Marketplace or buyer-side app
- Anything that adds a sixth product to the pitch before Stage 1's exit check is met
