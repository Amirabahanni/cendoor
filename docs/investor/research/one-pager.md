# Cendoor: one page

**The problem.** Small Moroccan sellers who sell on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp lose money on cash-on-delivery orders that go silent or get refused, and spend evenings phoning customers to confirm. (Failure-rate claims of 20% to over 50% come from vendors selling confirmation tools, not independent data.)

**The customer.** One-person and small shops (1 to 5 people) selling fashion, beauty, perfume, food, home goods, mostly by DM. Best fit in the simulated panel: full-time solo sellers (25% would buy) and small shops (40%, thin sample) who phone customers to confirm.

**The offer and the price.** Cendoor confirms each order with the customer in Darija, French or Arabic before the seller ships, builds the store link from a chat, and tracks delivery and cash. The seller approves every message for the first 14 days. **Cendoor Founding 50:** first 50 sellers lock **99 MAD/month for 12 months** (standard **149 MAD**; **Growth 299 MAD**), 14-day free trial with no card, refund of the month if no real order is confirmed in 30 days, or **4 MAD per confirmed order** with no monthly fee (not yet modelled).

**The numbers** (MAD; estimates; no founder pay included):
| | |
|---|---:|
| Price (Solo) | 149 MAD/month |
| Contribution per paying seller | about 126 MAD/month (85%) |
| Profit margin at 100 paying sellers | 65% |
| Break-even | 24 paying sellers (39 at 99 MAD) |
| Year 1 operating profit (60 paying sellers by month 12) | +3,494 |
| Startup spend (estimate) | 19,000 |
| Year 1 after startup | -15,506 |
| Cash needed before it pays for itself | about 29,820 |
| Computed verdict | Profitable (narrow: see the plan) |

**The evidence.**
- **Simulated buyers (not real customers, upper bound):** 5 of 20 would buy the Founding 50 offer, up from 2 of 20 for the plain 149 MAD pitch. 8 of 15 who pass cite **trust**.
- **Board:** 3 fund-if, average 4.7/10; asks for 5 to 20 real sellers with measured results before more building.
- **Competitors (public pages):** eGrow from $59/month with 200+ stores claimed, YouCan Confirm, Babliy (Darija AI on WhatsApp, launched June 2026). Cendoor's price (149 MAD) is below all priced competitors; the open lane is the Instagram-only seller with no store (an inference to test).
- **No real seller has used it yet. No real test has been run.** That is the next step.

**What the money is for.** About 19,000 MAD to register the business, get legal advice on the customer-data law (CNDP), set up WhatsApp access and launch; about 2,000 MAD a month on marketing; hosting and messaging at about 23 MAD per seller plus 940 MAD a month fixed.

**The ask.** Introductions to 10 Moroccan sellers who confirm cash-on-delivery orders by hand, and 29,820 MAD of runway (plus an income for the founder) to run a 30-day pilot with 3 to 10 of them, then a written commitment to pay 99 MAD from at least 3.

*Simulated buyers and projected numbers are not customers or results. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
