These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing other sellers I know in my city using it for a few months and getting real results, plus a free trial where I can check it myself on a few real orders with no card needed.
- Proof from sellers like me that it cut refused and fake COD orders, plus a free trial where I can see it confirm real orders in Darija without losing customers.
- Seeing it work with a seller I know personally, and being able to read and approve every reply before it goes to a customer.
- Seeing other sellers I know personally using it for months with real results, and a trial where I keep control and confirm orders myself first.
- A free or very cheap plan I only pay for when it actually confirms orders, plus a chance to see it handle real customers and have me approve replies before they go out.
- Seeing it confirm real orders for another seller like me, in Darija, and cut my phone calls, with no monthly fee until it actually brings me confirmed orders.
- A free plan, or paying only per confirmed order (a small commission), so I pay nothing in a month with no sales.
- Pay per order only (a small cut per confirmed sale) or a free plan for small sellers, with no monthly fee.
- Seeing other sellers I know using it for months with real results, and being able to read every message it sends before it goes out during the trial.
- A cheaper plan, around 50 MAD a month or a small fee per order, and a free trial where I can see it handle my real WhatsApp customers in Darija.
- Proof from sellers like me that it cut refused or fake COD orders, plus a free trial where I can see it confirming real orders without costing me sales.
- Pay only per confirmed and delivered order with no monthly fee, plus I can read and approve every message before it goes out, and see other sellers I know using it without problems.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
