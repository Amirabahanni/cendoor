# Use of funds and the ask (draft for the founder to decide)

**The amount, the terms and the type of investment (equity, convertible, grant, loan) are the founder's decision.** What follows is a costed draft built from `research/numbers.json`, so that any figure you state is traceable. All costs are **estimates without quotes** (see `research/cfo-sources.md`). Amounts in MAD; 10 MAD = $1 is an assumption.

## What the CFO model says it takes

- Fixed costs: **2,940 MAD a month** (hosting 150, tools 100, accountant and data-protection reserve 400, WhatsApp provider fee 290, marketing 2,000).
- Startup spend: **19,000 MAD** (registration and accounting 6,000, legal advice 5,000, brand and domain 1,000, launch marketing and pilot incentives 5,000, WhatsApp onboarding 2,000).
- Break-even: **24 paying sellers** at 149 MAD (39 at 99 MAD), **71 paying sellers** if the founder takes 6,000 MAD a month.
- Cash needed before it pays for itself with the plan ramp and **no founder pay**: about **29,820 MAD**.

## What an investor will want to see before wiring money

1. A registered company and a clear cap table (`company-and-cap-table.md`).
2. Real pilot results (`metrics-and-kpis.md`): sellers, orders, before/after.
3. Written commitments from sellers at the stated price.
4. The legal basics for customer data and messaging (`legal/`).
5. A clear answer to "why you" (the team page of the deck: **to be completed by the founder**).

## What the money does NOT buy

WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Meta ads approvals are granted by those companies, not purchased. Real carrier tracking needs carrier agreements. Neither is guaranteed by funding.
