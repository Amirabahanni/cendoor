1 · In one page

What Cendoor is, and where it stands

A small seller in Morocco posts a reel, gets orders by DM, then loses evenings confirming them by phone, and loses money when cash-on-delivery parcels are refused. Cendoor takes over that work.

The problem

Sellers on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp take most orders by message and most payments in cash on delivery. They answer the same questions all day, phone customers to confirm, chase carriers, and have no simple shop. Orders that go silent or get refused cost them stock, shipping and time. (Published failure rates of 20% to over 50% come from vendors who sell confirmation tools, not from independent data.)

The solution

One link in the seller's bio opens a shop designed for them. A customer chat in Darija, French, Arabic or English takes the order. An AI agent confirms it with the customer before the seller ships. Delivery and cash are tracked, and the seller can send offers and launch ads from a button. The seller makes content and ships the parcel; Cendoor does the rest.

2 · The journey

From a post to a delivered order

Eight steps. The seller does the first and the last; the middle is automated.

The seller postsA reel, story or status with the Cendoor link in the bio.
The customer opens the shopA photo-led shop made for this seller, on any phone, in three languages.
The customer ordersBy quick form or by chatting, in Darija, French, Arabic or English.
The AI confirmsIt messages the customer to confirm; a risk check flags doubtful orders for the seller.
The seller approves and shipsLabel, carrier suggestion and stock update. The seller sees one list of tasks.
The customer is kept informedStatus messages and a tracking page; failed deliveries trigger a recovery message.
Cash is trackedWhich carrier delivered, which cash has been paid back to the seller.
The seller sells againTargeted offers to past customers and ads launched from a button, each with a tracked link.
3 · The product

What each part does

The dashboard has seven entries: Home, Studio, Orders, Inbox, Products, Customers, Settings. It is dark by default with an optional light mode.

AI shop builder and Studio Live in demo

  • Each shop is generated from a description: layout, colours, fonts, photos, sections.
  • The Studio is a chat: "make it dark with gold", "add an About section", "price of oud perfume 250".
  • Undo for every change; photo upload for logo, hero, products.
  • New sellers get category starter photos and sample products.
  • Free-form Claude-written pages cost credits and have not yet been run with a real key. Untested

Customer chat and orders Live in demo

  • Takes an order from start to finish: product, variant, name, phone, city, address.
  • Understands Darija, Arabizi, French, Arabic, English, Moroccan cities and phone formats.
  • Complaints are handed to the seller instead of answered by the bot.
  • The seller can take over any conversation.

Confirmation and risk Live in demo

  • Orders wait for the customer's "yes" before shipping.
  • A risk check (new customer, short address, past failures) marks orders green or red.
  • The seller approves the doubtful ones.
  • Cross-seller risk signal on hashed phone numbers: needs many sellers

Shipping, tracking and cash Simulated

  • Carrier suggestion by city and price (Amana, CTM, Aramex, CashPlus in the demo).
  • Customer tracking page with order number and phone.
  • Cash-on-delivery tracking and a carrier performance table.
  • Real carrier tracking needs carrier contracts.

Inbox Needs approval for live channels

  • One list for web, WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok conversations, with "needs you" flags.
  • Works in the demo; WhatsApp sending needs Meta business verification and approved templates; Instagram and TikTok inboxes need platform access.
  • Until then, messages are queued with a one-tap WhatsApp link.

Customers and campaigns Live in demo

  • Pick an audience by city, products, spend, orders, recency, channel; or use a quick audience (VIP, recent, win-back).
  • Write or generate the message; preview it for a real customer; confirm.
  • Safeguards: opt-out, 3 campaigns a day, 500 recipients, rate limit.
  • Each campaign has a tracked link with visits, orders and revenue.

Ads from a button Dry run until approved

  • The seller connects their own Meta ad account, picks a product, gets ad copy and sets a budget (20 to 300 MAD a day, max 14 days).
  • Cendoor creates campaign, ad set, creative and ad; the seller pays Meta directly.
  • The report counts delivered orders against the money spent.
  • Other sellers' accounts need Meta app review and business verification; TikTok ads are not built.

Post links and advice Live in demo

  • One tracked link per reel or story with visits and revenue.
  • Caption generator.
  • Copilot: stock runway, best post, customers to win back, carrier problems, failure rate.
  • Roles for a small team: admin, manager, operator, viewer.

Security and data

  • Ad-account tokens stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • Shop pages are sanitised so generated code cannot run unsafe scripts.
  • Roles limit what team members can do; customers can opt out of messages with STOP.
  • Cross-seller risk signals use hashed phone numbers only.
  • No external security audit yet; self-description only.

Legal and company

  • Customer data and marketing messages fall under Moroccan Law 09-08 (CNDP). Terms, privacy policy and data-processing texts are drafts that need a Moroccan lawyer.
  • The company is not registered yet, so there is no cap table, bank account or contracts.
  • Product photos in the demo come from Pexels and from the founder; sellers upload their own.
The five gaps an informed reader will notice: no customers or revenue; no registered company; team page not written; key integrations not live; no external security review and legal texts in draft. We name them first.
