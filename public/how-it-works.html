The complete description of the platform

Everything Cendoor does, in one page

Cendoor is an AI service for small Moroccan sellers on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. It builds the seller's shop from a chat, answers customers, takes and confirms orders, tracks deliveries and cash, and sends offers and ads. This page explains every part, shows the real screens, and says plainly what works today and what needs approval from someone else.

1 The shop Live in the demo

Each seller gets their own website at a link like /s/your-shop, built from a description, not from a template.

It is generated, not templated

The generator composes the page from independent parts (header, hero, value strip, product layout, About, reviews, FAQ, footer), each with several layouts. A palette is derived from the colours and mood the seller asks for, with one of 14 font pairings (Latin and Arabic), a shape language and its own artwork. Asking for "another version" gives a genuinely different site.

elegantluxuryminimalboldplayfulfuturisticnaturalwarm

What every shop includes

  • Product catalogue with variants (colour, size), price, stock and categories
  • Photos: hero, About, logo and every product
  • Announcement bar, reviews, FAQ, delivery and contact sections
  • Cash on delivery, delivery fee by city, free-shipping threshold
  • A chat button and a quick order form
  • Mobile first, right-to-left for Arabic, calm scroll animations

Photos and starter content

  • Sellers upload their own photos (hero, About, logo, each product)
  • A new seller who accepts sample products also gets matching starter photos for the category (home, beauty, food, fashion, shoes and bags, other)
  • The seller's own photos are never replaced
  • Products can be imported from a CSV file

Advanced AI design

Needs an AI key

For designs the generator cannot express, Claude can write a bespoke page through a strict safety filter. This uses AI credits (10 on the trial, 0 on Solo, 100 a month on Growth). Everyday customisation never uses credits. This mode has not yet run against a real key.

2 The Studio: run the shop by talking Live in the demo

A chat assistant that changes the shop, the products and the customers' messages from plain sentences, in Arabic, French, English or Darija, by typing or by voice. Every change can be undone.

What you can say

  • "Build my website: elegant abayas, black and gold"
  • "Another version" / "use a carousel for products" / "big banner"
  • "Make it dark" / "serif font" / "use gold and green"
  • "Hero title: The caftan season is here"
  • "Add an About section: ..." / "add FAQ: question? answer"
  • "Add my Instagram" / "use this photo as my main photo"
  • "Add product: black abaya 350 dirham 12 pieces"
  • "Add 10 to the perfume" / "change the price of the bag to 390"
  • "How much did I sell today?"
  • "Send an offer to customers in Rabat who spent over 300"
  • "Undo"

Rules first, AI when needed

The built-in understanding handles the common requests instantly and for free. Only requests it cannot understand go to Claude, and only if the shop has AI credits and a key is set. A failed AI call costs nothing.

Edit by hand too

"Edit text and photos" lets the seller change the name, tagline, announcement bar, hero text, About, footer, contact links and photos directly, free.

Always reversible

Every Studio change is logged and can be undone, including a build or the sample products.

3 Customer chat and orders Live in the demo

Customers can order from the shop's chat, from the quick form, or (once connected) on WhatsApp. The assistant takes the whole order and answers questions about products, price, stock and delivery.

What the assistant understands

  • Darija, Arabizi, French, Arabic and English, mixed in one message
  • Moroccan cities, phone formats (06, 07, +212), addresses
  • Products by name, nickname or category; sizes and colours; quantities
  • "How much?", "do you have it in black?", "when will it arrive?"
  • Voice input in the shop chat where the browser supports it

What it does

  • Collects product, variant, name, phone, city and address, one question at a time
  • Quotes the total with the delivery fee for that city
  • Creates the order, reserves stock and links it to the conversation
  • Passes the conversation to the seller when it is unsure
Honest status: the web chat works end to end. The same conversations on WhatsApp need a verified business and approved templates from Meta; Instagram and TikTok messages need platform approval. Until then every message is prepared for the seller to send with one tap. Voice notes sent by customers are flagged for the seller, not transcribed.

4 Order confirmation and risk Live in the demo

Cash on delivery fails when a customer orders and then goes silent or refuses the parcel. Cendoor checks each order and asks the customer to confirm before the seller ships.

The confirmation agent

Live
  • An order from the quick form waits as "pending confirmation" and starts a conversation
  • The AI messages the customer: order number, total, city, "reply YES to confirm"
  • YES confirms the order automatically; NO cancels it and restores stock
  • The seller sees what is waiting on the Home screen and can approve by hand
  • It can be switched off in Settings

The risk check

Live

Every order gets a green, yellow or red risk level from visible reasons:

  • invalid phone number, new customer, short address
  • this customer's past failed or delivered orders
  • a duplicate order in the last hours, a very high amount
  • the shared signal from other sellers (below)

Cendoor Shield

Needs many sellers

A scrambled (hashed) version of a customer's phone number with counts of delivered and failed orders, shared across sellers. No names, addresses or seller identities are stored or shown. With few sellers it carries little information.

Approve before send

Planned

For the first days, every message to a customer waits for the seller's tap, with an always-approve option and a per-shop kill switch. Not built yet; it is the first item in the roadmap because trust is the top objection from sellers.

5 Orders and shipping Live in the demo

What the seller can do

  • See all orders, filter by status (to approve, confirmed, shipped, delivered, failed, cancelled), search
  • Create an order by hand (a customer who phoned or wrote privately)
  • Confirm, ship, mark delivered or failed, cancel (stock is restored)
  • Print a shipping label (A6, with a barcode of the tracking number)
  • Export all orders to a CSV file
  • One stock across every channel, with low-stock alerts

Carriers

Amana, CTM, Aramex and CashPlus come set up, with a base price and per-city prices. The seller can edit them.

Automatic carrier choice

Cendoor learns from the seller's own history which carrier delivers best to each city and can choose it automatically.

Products and stock

Variants with their own stock, price changes by sentence, CSV import, archive and restock, and a "running low" warning with how long it will last.

Honest status: the carrier's real tracking feed is simulated by a button in the demo. Live carrier status needs each carrier's API access.

6 Tracking, cash and failed deliveries

For the customer Live

  • A tracking page that needs both the order number and the phone number used, so it is private
  • The steps of the order from confirmed to delivered, in the customer's language
  • Automatic status messages with the tracking link (prepared for the seller to send until WhatsApp is connected)

For the seller Live

  • Cash and delivery screen: what each carrier owes, what has been remitted, what is still pending
  • Delivery success rate and refused orders
  • Failed-delivery recovery: the AI asks the customer why, offers to redeliver or cancel, and restocks if cancelled
  • A review request after a delivered order

7 The inbox Live in the demo

How it works

  • Web chat conversations (and WhatsApp ones once connected) appear in one list
  • The AI answers; the seller can read everything in real time
  • Take over any conversation: the AI pauses and the seller types
  • Conversations that need a human are flagged (unclear message, voice note, upset customer)
  • Unread counts show on the menu

8 Customers and offers Live in the demo

Every customer who orders is saved with their orders, spend and delivery history. The seller can send an offer or an announcement to exactly the right customers, and always sees who will get it and the exact message first.

Targeting

  • Quick audiences: all, best customers, ordered recently, lapsed, first-time buyers
  • Fine-tune by city, product or category bought, total spent, number of orders, last order date, the channel they ordered from, and reliable delivery history
  • Live count and sample names; save an audience to reuse

Writing

One-tap drafts in three languages (offer, new arrivals, free delivery, back in stock, thank you, win-back). Personalise with {name}, {city}, {last_product} and a tracked {link}.

Safety

Preview and confirmation before sending. Customers who reply STOP are excluded for good. At most 3 campaigns a day and 500 recipients each.

Results

Every campaign gets its own tracked link: the seller sees the visits, orders and revenue it brought in.

Honest status: sending through WhatsApp needs approval from Meta and approved message templates. Until then each message is queued with a one-tap WhatsApp link for the seller. Moroccan data-protection law (09-08) requires the customer's consent for marketing messages and a declaration to the CNDP; the legal drafts are in the documentation pack and must be reviewed by a lawyer.

9 Ads from one button Dry run in the demo Needs Meta approval

A seller who already advertises on Meta connects their own ad account, picks a product, reviews the ad and the exact budget, confirms, and Cendoor creates the campaign in their account. The seller pays Meta directly; Cendoor never holds ad money.

The safeguards

  • Nothing is created without an explicit confirmation
  • 20 to 300 MAD a day, up to 14 days, up to 2,000 MAD in total, 3 running ads
  • Everything is created paused and switched on only when all parts exist; if Meta refuses halfway, nothing is left running
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted
  • Each ad gets a tracked link; the report counts delivered orders against the budget that has run
Honest status: the flow runs fully in the demo as a dry run. The calls to Meta follow its documented format but have never run against Meta's live servers. Connecting other sellers' accounts needs a Meta app approved by Meta (app review and business verification); before that only people added to the app can connect, which is enough for a pilot. TikTok ads and WhatsApp-click ads are not built.

12 Security and data

What is protected

  • Passwords hashed with scrypt and a per-user salt; sessions are random 256-bit tokens in a secure cookie
  • Role-based permissions on every action
  • Rate limits on login, signup, chat, orders, tracking, campaigns and ads
  • Generated websites pass a strict allow-list filter: no scripts, forms or remote images
  • Ad-account tokens encrypted at rest; phone numbers hashed in the shared fraud signal
  • The customer can reply STOP at any time

What is not in place yet

  • No external security audit or penetration test
  • No two-factor authentication and no audit log
  • Single small server; automated backups still to be added
  • The company is not registered, the data-protection (CNDP) declaration is not filed, and the terms and privacy policy are drafts for a lawyer

More detail is in the security overview and the legal drafts of the documentation pack. The seller owns their shop and customer data; Cendoor processes it on the seller's behalf.

? Questions

Is this live with WhatsApp?

Not yet. WhatsApp needs a verified business and approved message templates from Meta. Today the web chat is live and every outgoing message is prepared with a one-tap WhatsApp link for the seller.

Does the AI understand Darija?

The built-in understanding handles common Darija and Arabizi ordering phrases. How well it copes with real, messy conversations is exactly what the pilot will measure. Voice notes are flagged for the seller, not transcribed.

What if the AI says something wrong to a customer?

The seller can read every conversation and take over. A mode where every message waits for the seller's tap, with a kill switch, is planned first. The seller stays responsible for messages sent in their name.

Is the website a template?

No. Each shop is generated from the description, from independent parts, palettes and fonts, so another description gives a genuinely different site. Photos make it the seller's own.

Who owns the customer data?

The seller. Cendoor processes it on the seller's behalf. The shared fraud signal stores only a scrambled phone number and counts, never names or addresses.

How does the seller pay for ads?

Directly to Meta, from the seller's own ad account. Cendoor never holds ad money and enforces a budget cap and a confirmation.

What does it cost?

14 days free, then 149 MAD a month (Solo) or 299 MAD (Growth). The first 50 sellers can lock 99 MAD for 12 months.

Who else does this?

eGrow and YouCan Confirm sell order confirmation, and Babliy launched a Darija WhatsApp agent in 2026. Cendoor's difference is building the shop from a chat for a seller who only has an Instagram or TikTok account, at a lower price. That claim is an inference from their public pages and needs confirming with sellers.
