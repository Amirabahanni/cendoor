What Shopify and a COD app cannot do for a chat seller

Four things built for how Moroccan sellers actually work: from a DM to a delivered parcel.

Talk to your shop

Say "make my shop green", "announce free delivery this weekend" or "lower the caftan to 600" in Darija, French or English. The storefront changes live, and every change can be undone. No dashboard to learn.

Cendoor Shield

Failed cash-on-delivery is the seller's biggest loss. Cendoor shares delivery outcomes between sellers as anonymous counters, so a number that refused parcels elsewhere is flagged before you print a label.

Cendoor Copilot

"CTM delivers 94% of your parcels to Rabat, CashPlus 61%." "Blue Satin M sells out in 3 days: reorder 30." It reads your own orders and tells you what to do, and can pick the best carrier for each city for you.

One link per post

Every reel, story or video gets its own link and a ready-to-paste caption in Darija, so you see which post really sells. Customers can track their parcel on a public page, which cuts "where is my order?" messages.

What works today, and what is next

This is an early product. Here is the honest picture.

Working now

  • Shop link with per-post tracking links
  • AI chat in Darija (Latin and Arabic script), French, English, with voice input and read-aloud
  • Orders from chat, shared stock, COD risk score and Cendoor Shield (cross-seller delivery signal)
  • Studio: change your shop by talking to it, with live preview and undo
  • Copilot insights, automatic best-carrier choice, post kit captions, public order tracking
  • Printable labels with barcode
  • Status updates to the customer, reviews, failed-delivery recovery
  • Cash reconciliation, team roles, three-language dashboard

Needs approvals or partners

  • Two-way WhatsApp and Instagram connection: needs Meta business verification
  • TikTok messages: TikTok limits this to approved partners
  • Live carrier tracking: needs each carrier's API access (carrier updates are simulated in the demo)
  • Online billing: not connected yet
  • Cendoor Shield only gets strong with many sellers: the demo uses fictional data from other sellers

Questions

Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. They tap your link, chat or fill a short form, and pay cash on delivery.

Does it replace WhatsApp?

No. Customers can start a WhatsApp chat from your links. Once Meta approves the connection, the same AI answers there too.

Can the AI make mistakes?

It only quotes prices and stock from your catalogue, asks the customer to confirm every order summary, and hands the chat to you when it is unsure or when a customer is unhappy. You can pause it on any conversation.

Where is customer data stored?

In your Cendoor account. Moroccan data protection rules (Law 09-08) apply to customer details; get legal advice before launching commercially.
