Investor guide · 6 minutes

Try Cendoor as a seller and as a customer.

Everything runs on fictional demo data. Nothing you create is kept: a reset button in Settings returns the demo to its original state.

  1. Describe a website, get a different one each time

    Open the dashboard with the demo login below. In Studio, say Build my website and describe it: abayas, luxurious, black and gold. Then another version, use a carousel for products, make it dark. Each description and each version is a different layout, palette and font pairing, not a template. Open the Edit text & photos tab to change the words and upload pictures by hand. Every change has an Undo.

  2. Be the customer

    Open the demo shop, tap “Chat to order”, write bghit robe zerqa M (Darija), Je veux une abaya noire or I want a dress, and finish the order. The microphone works in Chrome and Edge.

  3. Run the order

    In the dashboard, open Orders: approve, print the label, mark it handed over, then press “simulate next carrier update”. The customer chat receives each update, with a tracking link.

  4. Send an announcement

    In Studio type send a message to customers who haven't ordered in 2 months. You see the audience and the exact text first, and nothing is sent until you reply send. (Customers who replied STOP are excluded. WhatsApp is not connected in the demo, so messages are queued with one-tap links under Customers > Campaigns.)

  5. Trigger the fraud shield

    Place an order with the phone number 0670000099. Other (fictional) sellers reported failed deliveries for it, so Cendoor holds the order for approval.

  6. Read the advice

    The Home screen shows what the AI learned from the seller’s own orders (best carrier per city, stock run-out, best-converting post) and the cash still held by carriers.

  7. Track as a customer

    Open the tracking page with order CD-0102 and phone 0673141516. Switch the dashboard to Arabic to see right-to-left support.

What is real, and what is not yet

Working now

  • AI that designs a different website for each description (layout, palette, fonts, illustration), with photo and text editing and undo
  • AI confirmation agent and announcements to customers, with confirmation, opt-out and a daily limit
  • Customer chat in Arabic, French, English and Darija, with voice
  • Orders, shared stock, labels, delivery updates, failed-delivery recovery
  • Fraud shield, carrier advice, cash tracking, team roles
  • 14-day free trial flow

Needs approvals or partners

  • Two-way WhatsApp and Instagram: needs Meta business verification
  • TikTok messages: limited to approved partners
  • Live carrier tracking: carrier APIs (simulated in the demo)
  • Payments and billing: not connected yet
  • Fully bespoke AI-written pages: need an AI key and use credits (basic customization is free and unlimited)
  • The fraud shield only becomes strong with many sellers (demo data is fictional)
