The platform

One AI that runs your online shop.

Cendoor is built for sellers who sell on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. You describe your business. It designs the website, answers your customers, takes and confirms the orders, follows every delivery and every dirham, and sends your announcements. You post and ship.

Post→Customer chats→AI takes the order→AI confirms it→You ship→Updates and cash tracked

1. Your website, designed by the AI

Describe it in a sentence. Every description produces a different site, not a template with new colours.

Built from a description Live

“A luxurious website for my abayas, black and gold.” The generator composes layout, palette, fonts and illustration for that brief. Eight moods: elegant, luxury, minimal, bold, playful, futuristic, natural, warm.

Change it by talking Live

“Another version”, “make it dark”, “use gold and green”, “serif font”, “use a carousel for products”, “big banner”. Every change can be undone.

Your own text and photos Live

Edit the name, tagline, banner, About, footer and contact links by hand, and upload a logo, a main photo and an About photo. Send a photo in the chat to set it for you.

Real shop inside Live

Products, categories, best sellers, reviews, FAQ, delivery information and an order chat are placed in your design automatically and stay up to date.

Arabic, French, English Live

Visitors switch language in one tap, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and self-hosted Arabic and Latin fonts.

Fully bespoke pages Needs an AI key

For designs the generator cannot express, Claude can write a custom page for you. This uses AI credits (see plans). Basic customisation never does.

2. Customers: replies, orders and confirmation

Customers order from your link in the language they write. Nobody has to type the same answer twice.

AI replies, day and night Live

Answers questions about price, colour, size, stock and delivery in Arabic, French, English and Moroccan Darija. It only quotes prices and stock from your catalogue.

Voice Live

Customers can speak their order and hear replies; you can dictate a new product. Works in Chrome and Edge.

Orders from chat Live

The AI collects name, phone, city and address, shows a summary, and creates the order after the customer says yes. One shared stock, so no overselling.

Confirmation agent Live

Orders placed with the quick form wait until the customer replies YES to the AI’s message. NO cancels and restocks. Sent over WhatsApp once connected; a one-tap link until then.

Cash-on-delivery risk check Live

Each order gets a safe, check or risky label. Risky orders wait for your approval before a label is printed. Cendoor Shield adds anonymous delivery history from other sellers.

Hand-over to you Live

When a customer is unhappy or asks for the seller, the conversation is flagged in your inbox and the AI stops. You can reply and resume the AI whenever you like.

3. Delivery and money

From the label to the cash in your hand.

Labels and tracking Live

Printable shipping labels with a barcode. Customers get delivery updates and a public tracking page (order number plus phone).

Failed-delivery recovery Live

If a delivery fails, the AI offers the customer a retry tomorrow, pickup at the agency or cancellation, and acts on the answer.

Cash tracking Live

See what each carrier collected and what is still owed to you, and tick it off when it arrives. A daily report is one tap away.

Carrier advice Live

Cendoor learns from your own orders which carrier really delivers best per city, and can choose it automatically.

Live carrier tracking Needs carrier access

Real status feeds need each carrier’s API. The demo simulates the updates; the integration point is ready.

WhatsApp messages Needs Meta approval

Two-way WhatsApp (confirmations, updates, announcements) needs a verified Meta business account. Until then messages are recorded and queued with one-tap links.

4. Growth: announcements and insight

Tell customers what is new, and learn what works.

Targeted offers and announcements Live

Pick exactly who gets an offer: customers in Rabat who bought a given product and spent over 500 MAD, people who have not ordered in 2 months, first-time buyers, or anyone who ordered from TikTok. Save the audience, or just say it in the Studio chat. Each message is personalised with the customer’s name, city and last product, carries a tracked link, and you see the visits, orders and revenue it brought. You see the audience and the exact text first, and nothing leaves until you confirm. Customers who reply STOP are never messaged again, and there is a daily limit.

One link per post Live

Each reel, story or video gets its own tracked link and a ready-to-paste caption, so you see which post really sells.

Copilot Live

Plain advice from your own data: what is about to sell out and how much to reorder, your best-converting post, customers to win back, and what Cendoor handled for you this month.

Customers and reviews Live

Customer list with lifetime value, win-back suggestions, and a review request after every delivery, shown on your site.

5. Team, safety and plans

Roles Live

Admin, manager, operator and viewer, enforced on the server.

Safe generated pages Live

Everything an AI or a seller writes is filtered before it is shown: no scripts, forms, remote images or overlays. Customer data stays in your account.

Dashboard in three languages Live

Arabic (right-to-left), French and English, on phone or computer.
